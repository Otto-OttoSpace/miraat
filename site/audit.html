What quietly breaks in Arabic

Critical

Shattered letter-joins

letter-spacing / tracking cuts Arabic's cursive connections — the #1 cause of "broken-looking" Arabic.

Critical

Fonts faking Arabic

No Arabic family in the stack → the browser draws Arabic in a Latin/mono fallback (tofu, disconnected letters).

Major

Wrong mirroring

Physical CSS (margin-left, text-align:left, float) and hard-coded dir don't flip — layouts break in RTL.

Major

Numerals & bidi

Western digits in Arabic strings, corrupted mixed number/text runs — reads as half-translated.
